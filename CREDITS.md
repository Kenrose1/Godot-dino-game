# Credits

## Tileset, background, props and collectibles

**Free Platformer Game Tileset** by **pzUH** — <https://opengameart.org/content/free-platformer-game-tileset>
Licence: **CC0 (public domain)**. Mirror: <https://www.gameart2d.com/free-platformer-game-tileset.html>

Used for the terrain tiles (18 tiles), the scrolling background, and the
decorations and pickups: trees, bushes, stumps, signs, both mushrooms, the
crate and the stone. The enemy is the stone object from this same pack, so no
third-party enemy art was added.

## Player character

**"Lil practice walk cycle made in Procreate. Based on the Carnotaurus from
Prehistoric Planet"** by **u/BlazetheBean** on r/animation —
<https://www.reddit.com/r/animation/comments/zzqndm/lil_practice_walk_cycle_made_in_procreate_based/>
(the link printed in the lab manual, `…/comments/zzondm/…`, is a typo).

The 12 frames of the original GIF were extracted, keyed off the painted
background and sliced into the `default`, `running` and `jumping` animations,
as the lab instructs. This is the artist's own artwork: credit them as above in
the assignment submission.

## Tools used for the build

* Godot Engine 4.4.1 (MIT) — engine and headless build/verification.
* Python 3 + Pillow + NumPy/SciPy — asset slicing, background keying and the
  level preview renderer.
